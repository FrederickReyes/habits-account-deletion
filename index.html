Account deletion

Delete your Habits account

You can permanently delete your Habits account and the personal data associated with it directly from the Habits app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Habits app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open your Profile or account settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

What happens when you delete your account?

Habits will delete your account and the personal data associated with it from its active systems as part of the account deletion process.

This may include your account information and the habit, progress, and profile data associated with your account.

Data retention

Habits does not intend to retain your personal account data after a completed account deletion request, except where retention is required or permitted by applicable law, security requirements, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Where information must be retained for one of these reasons, it will be retained only for the period necessary for that purpose and handled according to applicable requirements.

Need help? If you cannot access your account or cannot complete the deletion process in the app, contact the Habits support team through the support contact provided in the app.
